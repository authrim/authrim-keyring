Authrim Keyring keeps the two-factor authentication codes for all your accounts in one place: private, quick to reach, and under your control.

ADD AN ACCOUNT IN SECONDS
• Scan the QR code a service shows when you turn on two-factor authentication.
• Or choose a screenshot of that QR code from your photos.
• Or type the setup key by hand.

YOUR CODES, ONE TAP AWAY
• Each account is a card. Tap it to see the current code and how long it stays valid.
• Tap the code to copy it. Copied codes stay on this device and are cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds.
• Give each card its own color. The text switches between black and white to stay readable.
• Star your favorites to keep them on top, swipe to switch between credentials, passkeys, and codes, and search by name, website, or tag.

FILLED IN FOR YOU
• Turn on Keyring in Settings > General > AutoFill & Passwords, and the code is suggested above the keyboard on the websites you set for each card.
• Save passkeys to Keyring and sign in with Face ID. Passkeys sync with iCloud Keychain when sync is on, or stay on this iPhone only for the websites you choose.

MOVE IN EASILY
• Scan the transfer QR codes from Google Authenticator.
• Import unencrypted exports from Aegis, 2FAS, Bitwarden, and Ente Auth, or a list of otpauth:// links.
• On iOS 26, bring codes and passkeys over from apps that support the system's export, such as Passwords, and send them to another app the same way.

RECOVERY CODES, KEPT WITH THEIR CARD
• Paste the recovery codes a service gives you. They stay hidden until Face ID, and you can mark each one as used.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Secret keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.
• Lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode.
• No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Keyring does not collect any data.
• Codes are generated on your device and work without an internet connection.

READY FOR A NEW DEVICE
• Turn on iCloud Keychain sync to have your accounts on your other devices. iCloud Keychain is end-to-end encrypted. Sync is off unless you turn it on.
• Export an encrypted backup file protected by a password you choose, and restore it on any device.

WORKS WITH YOUR SERVICES
Keyring works with any service that supports authenticator apps for two-factor authentication (TOTP, RFC 6238). It supports SHA-1, SHA-256, and SHA-512, 6- to 8-digit codes, periods from 10 to 300 seconds, counter-based codes (HOTP, RFC 4226), and Steam Guard codes.

Available in English and Japanese.
